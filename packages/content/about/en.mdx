I grew up in Vila Nova, in the municipality of Caraí, Minas Gerais. In 2015 I came to Finland following my mother, still young — I was 15. I finished lower secondary school in Finland, then studied a few years of lukio, and eventually decided to go to an ammattikoulu to learn a profession that interested me. Here I am today.

I studied software development at Savo Vocational College. During my studies, I started building WordPress sites for a few people, out of curiosity.
I learned to code and build projects on my own — free sites for friends, learning by doing. What interests me most is having real responsibility — for my own projects, and for the business of the people I work with.

That's also why this site is translated into three languages: I'm Brazilian and I've lived in Finland for over 10 years. I learned Finnish from scratch, and today I speak three languages fluently — Portuguese is my mother tongue, Finnish is the language I built my life in Finland with, and English is where most of the work happens. I switch between the three every day, all the time.
