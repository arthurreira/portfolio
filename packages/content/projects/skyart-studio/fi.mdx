## What I built

A professional website for a Finnish art studio client. The site showcases their artwork in a gallery, explains the services they offer (commissions, workshops), and gives potential clients a way to get in touch. Responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile. Built on WordPress with custom CSS for the visual style, Contact Form 7 for the inquiry form, and Yoast SEO for basic search optimisation.

## What I learned

Client work adds a constraint that personal projects don't have: someone else's requirements, preferences, and timeline. Understanding what the client actually needed (vs what they asked for) was the real skill. A gallery that loads fast matters more than one that looks impressive in a demo. I also learned how much of a professional WordPress site is configuration and plugin management rather than code — and why that's sometimes exactly the right approach. Clients who can update their own content without a developer are a better outcome than a custom-coded site they can't maintain.
