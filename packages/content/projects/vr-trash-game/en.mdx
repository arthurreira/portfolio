**VR Trash Game** is an immersive virtual reality experience designed to support physical rehabilitation.  
It focuses on improving **motor skills**, **hand-eye coordination**, and **strength recovery** through interactive gameplay that mimics everyday movements.  

Built for home use, the game combines therapy and entertainment, offering a safe and motivating environment where users can rebuild mobility at their own pace.
