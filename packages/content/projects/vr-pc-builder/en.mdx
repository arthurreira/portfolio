**PC Builder VR** is a collaborative VR game developed with **Unity** and the **Universal Render Pipeline (URP)**.  

Players can explore and assemble computer parts in virtual reality, creating custom PCs in an interactive environment.  

I contributed as a developer:  
- Optimizing gameplay performance and adding 3D objects  
- Modeling PC components with **Blender**  
- Assisting with UI and Unity setup  

Full project: [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/SakkyTT/VRGame3URP)
